Geode Grove: delete your data

Geode Grove only stores data if you use the optional online leaderboard: the nickname you typed, your score, the date, and a one-way hash of a random device id. There are no accounts.

To delete it, email support@tinyhousegames.com with the subject "Geode Grove data deletion" and the leaderboard nickname you used. We remove your nickname, scores and device hash from the leaderboard within 30 days and reply when it's done. Nothing is kept afterward.

Game progress is saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Ad data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google; see how Google uses data.
