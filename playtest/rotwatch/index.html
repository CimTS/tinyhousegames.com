Controls & field notes ↓

YOUR FIRST DEFENSE

Tap. Build. Hold.

Tap a glowing pad, pick a tower, then press Start Wave. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it. Level 3 is a choice between two paths.

The strip above the buttons shows what the next wave brings. 2x speeds things up; II pauses and holds sound and blood settings.

KEYBOARD

Keep your eyes on the gate.

With a pad selected, 1–4 build · U upgrades · Space starts a wave · F changes speed · Esc pauses · M changes volume.

Audio begins after your first tap.
