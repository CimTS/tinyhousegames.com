Controls & field notes ↓

YOUR FIRST DEFENSE

Choose. Build. Survive.

Choose a tower, then tap a glowing + pad. Press Start Wave when ready. The in-game Help button replays the tutorial.

Mouse or touch to build. On portrait screens, drag the battlefield to pan. Overview shows the entire route; Detail View restores the larger tactical view. Options shows tower stats, sound, reduced blood and speed.

KEYBOARD / AUDIO

Keep your eyes on the gate.

1–4 select towers · Space starts a wave · U upgrades · Esc pauses · M changes volume.

Audio begins after interacting with the game. Use Options or Pause to lower or mute sound, change speed, or reduce blood effects.
