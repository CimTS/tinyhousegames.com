Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.

Geode Grove online leaderboard: optional; stores only the nickname you type, your score, the date, and a salted one-way hash of a random device id, on Cloudflare; no IP addresses are stored; nicknames are filtered.

Ads: Geode Grove shows an optional rewarded ad for one extra life via Google AdMob. Google may collect device info and the advertising ID; link; consent is asked where required (UMP) and you can change it in the game; ads are limited to G/PG content.

Children: games are family-friendly; we don't knowingly collect personal info from children; parents can contact us.
