✦SLOTCHINKO

Draw a poker hand. Drop it through the pegs. Beat eight blinds.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hold and draw. Tap cards to keep them. You get two draws. Better hands drop more balls with more power.
  2. Drop. Press Drop Hand, or drag on the board to aim and release. Each ball scores by the pocket it lands in.
  3. Beat the blind. Your score carries over. It must reach the blind target after each drop, or the run ends.
  4. Shop. Coins buy Chips, which last the whole run, and one-shot items for your bag. Two different Chips with matching tags form a set.

Your most common suit sets the ball type: ♥ splits · ♠ heavy, breaks pegs · ♦ bonus bucket value · ♣ sticky.

Sets: heart + split · diamond + fortune · spade + gravity · club + control · economy + combo · element + synergy.

From blind 3 the outer pockets are drains that pay nothing. Clear all eight blinds to win.

Keyboard: 1 to 5 hold · D draw · Enter drop · arrows aim.
