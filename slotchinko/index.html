HOW TO PLAY

  1. Keep. Tap the cards you want to keep.
  2. Draw. Swap the rest. You can do that twice.
  3. Drop. Press Drop Hand, pick a spot on the board, then tap Drop. Each ball scores the number on the pocket it lands in.
  4. Win the round. You get three hands to reach the BLIND number. Then spend your coins in the shop.

MORE RULES

Rounds: every blind starts from zero. Each hand you don't need pays 1¢. Miss with your last hand and the run ends. Clear all 16 blinds to win.

Your most common suit sets the ball type, and where to aim: ♥ splits in two · ♠ heavy: the two outer pockets pay ×4 for it, even when they are drains · ♦ fortune: every pocket pays 40% more · ♣ sticky: lands close to where you aim.

Boards: from blind 6 the jackpot can sit left or right, or split into two smaller jackpots. The path screen names the next board, and the jackpot wears a gold cap.

Shop: Chips last the whole run. Bag items are used once. Tap a Chip you own, or View All, to see what it does or sell it for half its price.

Sets: two different Chips with matching tags form a set: heart + split · diamond + fortune · spade + gravity · club + control · economy + combo · element + synergy.

Drains: from blind 3 the two outer pockets pay nothing.

Bosses: blinds 4, 8, 12 and 16 have a boss with its own rule, shown on the path. Beat one to keep its trophy for the run. Trophies take no Chip slot.

Path: after each shop pick a plain blind, an Elite (harder, pays a Rare Chip), an Event or the Lounge. Milestones unlock new decks and items (see Collection). Win to open Ascension.
